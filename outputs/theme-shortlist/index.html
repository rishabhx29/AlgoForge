Pattern Studio

My first choice. Learning becomes the visual identity: warm graphite, clay-red diagrams, generous typography. Distinctive because it is about algorithms.

Circuit Club

The energetic option. Athletic typography and session-based practice. Less browsing an endless list; more showing up for a useful daily training set.

Questline

The journey-driven option. A connected skill tree, chapter titles and earned milestones. The game feeling comes from progression, not floating fantasy decoration.

Focus Deck

The everyday-tool option. Blue-gray surfaces, a persistent workspace and notes beside the next problem. Its appeal is ownership and continuity, not spectacle.

Start with Pattern Studio.

It is the strongest match for your brief: recognizably AlgoForge, not a reskinned SaaS dashboard. Pair its visual language with a simple daily-session flow inspired by Circuit Club. Keep one visual system—do not mix four palettes. These are design hypotheses, not proven retention improvements.

Reference principles, not copies

What should bring someone back?

One obvious next problem, visible mastery, saved reasoning, and a satisfying stopping point. A memorable UI can invite a first visit. Useful progress and continuity must earn the next one. Keep streaks informative, not punitive.

Where the impressive part belongs

Put expression into pattern explainers, progression and completion moments. Keep reading and solving quiet. No floating orbs, generic glass tiles, autoplay backgrounds or endless entrances.
